Feedback, bug reports, tool suggestions, partnership questions, takedown requests — drop a message below and I'll get back to you. Usually within 2–3 days.

Send a message

Prefer email? You can also reach me directly at hello@iloveprompts.org.

Before you write

Suggesting a new tool? Include the AI platform name, a link to its documentation if it has public prompt syntax, and an example of the kind of output you'd want the tool to help generate.

Reporting a bug? Tell me which tool, what browser and device you're on, and what you expected vs what actually happened. A screenshot is gold.

Privacy request? You can read our full Privacy Policy first — the quick version is that we don't require accounts and don't store prompts on our servers, so in most cases there's very little personal data to retrieve or delete. Use this form to confirm and we'll handle anything that applies to your situation.

Response times

This is a solo project, so replies aren't instant. Most messages get a response within 2–3 business days. Bug reports and privacy requests are prioritised.
