Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 April 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how iloveprompts.org ("we", "us", "our", or "the Site") collects, uses, and protects information when you visit and use our free AI prompt generator tools. By using the Site, you agree to the practices described below.

Plain-language summary: We do not require accounts or logins. The prompt forms you fill in are processed in your browser and are not stored on our servers. We use Google Analytics to understand traffic, and we may display ads through Google AdSense, which uses cookies. You can opt out of personalised ads at any time.

1. Who we are

iloveprompts.org is an independent website that offers free, browser-based prompt generation tools for popular AI platforms such as ChatGPT, Claude, Midjourney, DALL·E, Stable Diffusion, Suno, Udio, Runway, and Pika. The Site is operated as a personal project and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or partnered with any of the AI platforms mentioned.

2. Information we collect

2.1 Information you provide to prompt generators

When you use one of our prompt generators (for example, entering a subject, style, or tone into a form), that information is processed in your browser. For most tools the generated prompt is produced locally in your browser using templates, and no content is sent to our servers.

For tools that use an AI model to generate or refine prompts (for example, the Prompt Improver), your input is sent to a third-party AI API provider (Anthropic) solely for the purpose of returning a response to you. We do not store a copy of the content you submit to these tools on our servers, and we do not use it to train any model.

2.2 Automatically collected information

Like most websites, we automatically collect certain information when you visit:

  • Your IP address (used to approximate country/region for analytics)
  • Browser type and version
  • Device type and operating system
  • Pages you visit on the Site and the order in which you visit them
  • The website that referred you to us, if any
  • Time and date of your visit

This information is collected by our hosting provider and by the analytics and advertising services described below.

2.3 Cookies and similar technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies (such as local storage) for the following purposes:

  • Essential cookies: to remember your interface preferences (for example, selected category tabs).
  • Analytics cookies: to understand how visitors use the Site.
  • Advertising cookies: to show relevant ads and measure their performance.

3. Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics, a web analytics service provided by Google LLC, to understand traffic patterns and improve the Site. Google Analytics uses cookies and collects information such as your IP address (anonymised), pages visited, and time spent on the Site. This information is aggregated and does not identify you personally.

You can opt out of Google Analytics by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

4. Google AdSense and advertising

We may display advertisements on the Site through Google AdSense. Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites.

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits to this Site and other sites on the Internet.
  • You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • You may also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info.

Third-party advertising networks may also collect information about your visits to this and other websites to provide advertisements about goods and services of interest to you. For more information about Google's privacy practices, see the Google Privacy Policy.

5. Third-party services

In addition to the services above, we use the following third parties to operate the Site:

  • Hosting: Netlify (and its CDN) delivers the Site's static files.
  • AI processing: For tools that involve real-time AI generation or refinement, prompts are forwarded to the Anthropic API. See the Anthropic Privacy Policy.
  • Analytics and ads: Google Analytics and Google AdSense, as described above.

6. How we use the information we collect

We use the information described in this Policy to:

  • Operate, maintain, and improve the Site and its tools
  • Understand how visitors interact with the Site
  • Display relevant advertising and measure its performance
  • Detect and prevent fraud, abuse, and security incidents
  • Comply with legal obligations

7. Legal bases (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, the legal bases on which we rely to process your information are:

  • Legitimate interests — to operate and improve the Site and to understand how it is used.
  • Consent — for non-essential cookies, including analytics and advertising cookies, where required by law. You can withdraw consent at any time via your browser settings or the opt-out links above.
  • Legal obligations — where we are required to process information to comply with the law.

8. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including:

  • The right to access a copy of the personal information we hold about you
  • The right to request correction or deletion of your personal information
  • The right to object to or restrict certain processing
  • The right to data portability
  • The right to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority

Because we do not require accounts and do not store the prompts you generate on our servers, the personal information we hold about any individual user is limited. If you would like to exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details on our Contact page.

9. California residents (CCPA/CPRA)

If you are a California resident, you have the right to know what personal information we collect, to request deletion of that information, and to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising. We do not sell personal information in the traditional sense, but the use of third-party advertising cookies (such as those used by Google AdSense) may be considered "sharing" under the CPRA. You can opt out of personalised advertising using the Google Ads Settings link above.

10. Children's privacy

The Site is not intended for children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will take steps to delete it.

11. Data retention

Aggregate analytics data is retained by Google Analytics according to our configured retention period. We do not retain the content of prompts you generate using tools that process locally in your browser. For tools that use an external AI API, content is processed in real time and is not stored by us.

12. Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect information transmitted through the Site, including serving all pages over HTTPS. However, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

13. International transfers

Our third-party service providers (including Google and Anthropic) are based in the United States, and information may be transferred to and processed in countries outside your country of residence. Where required by law, these transfers are protected by appropriate safeguards.

14. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Material changes will be indicated more prominently. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

15. Contact us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please reach out via our Contact page.
